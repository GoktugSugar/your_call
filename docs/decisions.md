## 2026-07-08 — Chapter 1, the tracks: the baby rage-quit opener, ten ordered trolleys, a choice-independent mocking imp, and the multiple-choice verb (feat/chapter-one-tracks)
Built Chapter 1 as the game's opening run (it replaces the earlier opening in `Opening.unity`, still what New Game loads): the crash → wake → imp-frame intro, then a secret baby rage-quit opener, then the ten trolley problems in order with the two light breathers interleaved, then a short closer. Reuses the existing systems throughout; the only genuinely new pieces are the multiple-choice verb, two small presentation additions, and a few on-model figures.
- **The imp is sharpened to mock you regardless of what you choose, and never grades a choice.** Every dilemma's reactions were written (and the two existing trolleys rewritten) so the imp is more openly sneering and needling, and his mockery is **independent of the choice**: he never approves, condemns, or treats any option as right, wrong, better, or worse. He mocks the agonizing, the speed, the predictability, the hypocrisy — never the choice's goodness — so the no-right-answer rule holds. The **one sanctioned exception** is the baby beat below. See `narrative.md`.
- **The baby rage-quit secret ending, as the very first beat.** A plain lever trolley: a baby on the straight track, one ordinary man on the branch, the imp's mask fully on and business as usual, with no hint it is different. Save the baby (pull, kill the man) and it resolves like any trolley and continues to the Lever; **kill the baby (do nothing) and the imp breaks character** — genuine fury, real swearing, calls the player a monster, quits on the spot, fades, and the credits roll. It is the game's **second choice-triggered ending** alongside shoot-the-imp, and the single place the choice-independent-mocking rule is deliberately broken (the one time nothing-is-a-wrong-answer finally hit a wrong answer). Built as a general `DilemmaChoice._endsGame` flag plus an optional ending hook on `DilemmaRunner`/`DilemmaBeat`, reusing the existing `Ending` + `CreditsRoll` untouched.
- **The ten trolleys, in order:** 1 the Lever (existing, 5 vs 1, sharpened copy), 2 the Marked Ones (existing branded wretches, sharpened), 3 the Sleeping (two asleep vs one awake in terror), 4 the Passenger (a murderer and an innocent together vs one innocent), 5 the Begging (one pleading in view vs more turned away, its plea shown as **floating ink text over the figure**), 6 the Dark Track (three visible vs an unknown number in the dark, with an **after-choice reveal that the dark track was empty the whole time**), 7 the Parent (one parent with children at home vs two unattached, tunable), 8 the Reformed and the Cruel (an old killer turned decent vs a man who never killed but is vicious now), 9 Someone You Loved (five strangers vs one you loved, the first lever's math unchanged), 10 the Full Track (the first **multiple-choice**: three tracks, a child, a pregnant woman, three strangers, no empty rail). Each is a new `Dilemma` asset; the baby and beats 1 to 9 are Binary on the reused lever, beat 10 is multiple-choice.
- **Multiple-choice verb built (was documented, not built).** New `MultipleChoiceResolver` (pick one of several, reflective stats over N, like binary) plus one line in `VerbResolvers` and a `MultipleChoice` value appended to `DilemmaVerb`. Its presenter is a new `ThreeTrackConsequence`: a three-track apparatus drawn in the ink style from the shared `Trolley`, `Figure` and fold-and-fall, owning its three diegetic pick options (click a track's label or press 1/2/3), so the loop needs no text fallback. `DilemmaStage`/`DilemmaRunner` stay generic; no verb branches.
- **One shared trolley station serves the whole chapter.** `TrolleyConsequence` now tears down and rebuilds per beat and reads per-`Dilemma` figure counts, branding, an optional per-track figure prefab (the baby), and an optional plea, so a single station runs the baby and beats 1 to 5, 7 to 9; the Dark Track and Full Track get their own stations. `Track` gained count-driven figure positions and paths; `Lever` gained `ResetSwitch()` for reuse.
- **New on-model figures (subclasses of `Figure`, reusing its ink/brand/fold/fade).** `Figure` was refactored to an extensible base (a virtual shape seam, multi-stroke) with its default stick figure unchanged. `BabyFigure` (a clearly-a-baby doodle) and `PregnantFigure` (a rounded-belly figure) draw only their shape; the three-track child is a scaled `Figure`. All minimal ink, no detailed or creepy faces. New `FloatingFigureText` floats the begging plea in ink over the figure (the menu-bark treatment, no bubble). New `DarkTrackConsequence` draws the shroud and plays the empty-track reveal.
- **Blue/red button and the last bear moved out of this chapter.** Both beats (and their stations) were removed from the opening/Chapter 1 sequence; they open a later part. They are not lost — the standalone `BlueRedButton.unity` and `BearDilemma.unity` scenes (and the **shoot-the-imp** secret ending on the bear) are untouched and still run.
- **Profile and stats.** The trolleys nudge the existing ethics axes silently (one/many, mercy/justice, ends/rules) per beat; the Dark Track and the two light breathers are stats-only (no `profileImpact`). Every beat ends on the seeded "how others chose" reveal; the seeds are placeholders that divide the crowd, to replace with real data (see `open-questions.md`).
- **Reused, verified.** Scripts compile clean (0 errors/warnings). The rebuilt `Opening.unity` (22 beats) was verified: the beat list, stations, presenters, the baby ending hook, and the full-track no-text-fallback are wired correctly, and each station was play-tested by screenshot — the intro (amber imp, Caveat box), the baby (baby vs man on the reused lever), the Marked Ones rebuilt on the same station (five red wretches, clean teardown), the Begging plea floating in ink, the Dark Track shroud over an empty main with three on the branch, and the Full Track (child, pregnant woman, three strangers, three pick labels), plus the ending's credits page. Player-facing copy carries no dashes; no fail state, no scoring, profile still silent. Updated `gameplay.md`, `narrative.md`, `tech.md`, and `open-questions.md`.

## 2026-07-08 — Design pivot: from a moral trial to the imp's game of no-right-answer questions (feat/design-pivot-docs)
**A docs-only reframe. No gameplay code, scenes, prefabs, or assets changed** — only the markdown in `docs/` and `CLAUDE.md`. **This supersedes the earlier "moral trial" framing throughout:** where older entries or copy describe the game as a trial of the player's ethics, or as a moral-dilemma-only experience, this entry governs.
- **Frame: trial → the imp's game.** The afterlife is no longer a courtroom or a trial of the player's morality. It is the imp's game: he runs the player (still the dead judge, now the one being *figured out* rather than judged) through an ordered stream of questions to work out **who he is** — the whole person, not just his ethics. The irony is kept but reframed: the man who spent his life sizing up everyone else is now the one being sized up. The DUI stays a light recurring gag (unchanged). The payoff still points at the player: who *you* are, read through this character.
- **Content rule widened.** The only criterion for a question is that it has **no right answer** *and* it **divides people or reveals something about the answerer**. Dropped the "moral dilemmas only" framing: all question types are in scope (would-you-rathers, preferences, taste, absurd hypotheticals, confessions, and genuine dilemmas). Questions with an obvious consensus answer are cut, because the "how others chose" reveal is dead if everyone picks the same thing.
- **Mix: mostly light, heavy as spikes.** The light and medium questions are the volume; heavy dilemmas are rare, spaced spikes, paced in waves so a heavy one lands harder after a run of light ones. Tone stays dark comedy but playful: the imp gives the profound and the idiotic the same ceremony, and his single voice ties the range together.
- **Profile broadened to a "who you are" portrait.** From moral-only axes to a small set of characterful axes where **neither pole is the good one** (first pass: head vs. heart, self vs. others, order vs. chaos, risk vs. safety, honest vs. kind; aim four to six, still open). Both heavy and light questions can nudge them; **not every question feeds the profile** (pure-taste questions exist just for the fun and the crowd stats). The earlier moral axes (one vs. many, mercy vs. justice, ends vs. rules, order vs. freedom) **fold in** as the ethics-flavoured part, still used by the heavy dilemmas. Still silent, never a running gauge. The rejected selfish↔selfless framing still stands (no axis where one pole is simply "the good person").
- **Stats promoted to the central hook.** "How others chose" is now a central pillar, front and centre: every question ends on the crowd split as a real beat. Still display-only and offline-first, seeded from real survey data then real players; the coordination gamble stays the one load-bearing place. Added an **optional, occasional guess-the-crowd layer**: now and then the imp makes the player guess whether they are with the crowd or the odd one out before the reveal.
- **Structure: linear, multiple profile-based endings.** One ordered run through a curated set of questions with the imp between them; the path is the same for everyone. It is **not** branching — the silent profile decides which of several endings the imp delivers (the secret shoot-the-imp ending is one). Replay value is answering differently for a different ending.
- **Multiple-choice added as a question type.** Kept the four verbs (binary, allocation, coordination gamble, self-sacrifice) for the heavy spikes and **added simple multiple-choice** (pick one of several) as the everyday type carrying the light/medium volume alongside binary. Documented as a to-build verb (a new resolver + presenter + one line in `VerbResolvers`); **not built in this pass.**
- **Every question stays animated.** Each question still gets an animated ink consequence, scaled by weight (a quick beat for a light one, a full scene for a heavy dilemma) — the game's edge over text-based question games.
- **Also fixed a stale palette line.** `vision.md`'s "one accent colour" pillar was brought in line with the already-settled palette rule (deliberate colour on a black-ink-on-white base; see 2026-07-07), which it predated.
- **Docs touched:** `CLAUDE.md` (summary, pillars 1/2/3/7/8, don'ts), `vision.md` (all sections), `narrative.md` (frame, protagonist, ending, the profile), `gameplay.md` (loop, verbs, escalation, the question-content rule, the how-others-chose hook, structure, endings), `art-and-ui.md` (animated-consequence note, profile-gauge wording), `tech.md` (data model, the profile/axes, the multiple-choice verb, seeded data), `README.md` (file descriptions), and `open-questions.md` (new open items). Implemented content (the opening's heavy dilemmas, the menu, the systems) is unchanged and now reads as the built heavy spikes of the wider run.
