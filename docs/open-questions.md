It **repositions once per dialogue box** (`ImpRoamer`), hopping springily to a fresh spot and then holding to deliver, always clear of content via bounds-based keep-outs. Hop feel, roam bounds, and minimum clearance are tunable and likely to be refined in playtesting.
- **Placeholder audio.** The background music (`Assets/Audio/Music/bgm_placeholder.mp3`) and the six imp voice blips (`Assets/Audio/SFX/ImpVoice/`) are placeholders to replace with a final track and a final imp voice. Level, pitch range, and blip cadence are exposed tunables (first pass).
- **Dialogue box vs. minimal UI.** The narration sits in a stylised hand-drawn ink frame that **floats clear of the bottom edge** (`DialogueBox`), rounder and cleaner with a controlled wobble, holding **centred** text in the **Caveat** handwriting font; long narration **pages by whole sentences** inside it. The one softening of the "no boxes" rule (see `art-and-ui.md`). The bottom offset, box size and corner rounding, the Caveat weight (currently SemiBold) and font size, and the typing speed are all a first pass, tunable — the font size and box sizing especially, since together they set how many sentences land in one box (and thus the page count).
- **Advance pacing.** Narration is now advanced by click or key, one dialogue box at a time (and the imp repositions per box). Whether some narration should auto-advance after a hold instead of always waiting for input, and the typing speed and page lengths, are a first pass to tune in playtesting.
